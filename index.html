Meet the doc

Inside Dr. Ulon Johnson's Swirl Lab

Deep in a lab that smells a lot like fresh espresso, Dr. Ulon Johnson spent too many late nights staring into his coffee cup. Every stir gave the same answer: swirl. Cream into coffee, coffee into cream, strangers into friends. He called it The Great Swirl, and he decided the whole internet should be in on it.

$ULON is the result: a community meme coin on Solana, launched fair on pump.fun, built around good vibes, good coffee, and good people mixing together.

1. The Mission

  • Grow the community: bring together meme lovers, coffee lovers, and the merely curious.
  • Get people mingling: online swirl sessions, meme contests, and, someday, real coffee meetups.
  • Spread awareness: share the Swirl far and wide, one meme and one cup at a time.

2. The Vision: an Ulon Swirl in every cup

Our dream is to one day see an "Ulon Swirl" flavor on real coffee menus. We're working toward building the community and infrastructure that could make partnerships with coffee shops possible, starting with local cafés and, as a big ambition, maybe even chains like Starbucks or Peet's someday.

To be clear: this is a goal, not a fact. $ULON has no partnership, sponsorship, or affiliation with Starbucks, Peet's, or any coffee brand today.
